ABOUT OUR PLATFORM

One Platform.
Multiple Opportunities.

The Campus Placement Portal is a web-based platform designed to connect students, companies and placement administrators in one place.

Students can discover jobs and apply online. Companies can post vacancies and manage applicants. Administrators can monitor and manage the complete placement process.
